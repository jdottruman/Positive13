Our Mission

The 13th Positive Foundation is dedicated to empowering youth and families through mentorship, education, and community engagement. We strive to bring positivity, hope, and unity to our neighborhoods by serving those in need and inspiring the next generation of leaders.

Our Core Values

Empowerment

We believe every young person deserves the tools and encouragement to achieve their full potential.

Community

We uplift one another through collaboration, compassion, and shared purpose.

Integrity

We lead with honesty, transparency, and respect in every action we take.

Legacy

We honor the generations before us by building a foundation that strengthens those who come after.

How We Live Our Mission

Mentorship

Pairing youth with dedicated mentors to build confidence, leadership skills, and direction for the future.

Community Outreach

From food and toy drives to local clean-ups, we extend kindness through service and action.

Education & Scholarships

Supporting students in their pursuit of learning through scholarships and academic initiatives.

Our Impact

Each year, we reach hundreds of families through community events, mentorship programs, and acts of service. Together, we are cultivating positivity and opportunity throughout the Proviso Township and beyond.
